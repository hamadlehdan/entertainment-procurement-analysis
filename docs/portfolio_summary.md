## Interview summary
I designed a reproducible procurement-intelligence analysis using actual government contract award notices. It classifies entertainment, event-production and AV-related notices, deduplicates repeated releases, and compares award values by buyer and service group while preserving direct evidence links. I distinguish public award values from actual supplier costs, realised spend, or savings, and explain why a qualitative supplier assessment is still necessary.

Source and script: official UK Contracts Finder OCDS feed and `src/analyse.py`.
